About Me

Hi! I'm Alyza, a 4th year student pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Multimedia Studies. I also do freelance work as a digital illustrator. In my free time, I enjoy creating digital art and playing games.

Things I Love

Studio Ghibli Films

I've enjoyed Studio Ghibli films since childhood, and my appreciation for them has continued throughout my teenage years and into adulthood. Beyond their beautiful colors, imaginative worlds, and creative storytelling, I've also grown interested in the amount of thought put into their animation. I learned that Hayao Miyazaki, one of the studio's most well-known directors, places great importance on portraying movement and everyday details realistically, even within fantastical settings. I believe this combination of realistic animation and magical storytelling is what makes these films feel so alive and distinguishes Studio Ghibli from other animated films.

Stardew Valley

I grew up playing pixel-art games, so Stardew Valley is something that naturally appeals to me. It's an ever-evolving farming simulation game that continues to introduce new content while allowing players to progress at their own pace. I enjoy completing tasks without the pressure of real-life responsibilities, especially because even the simplest activities in the game feel rewarding. From its pixel-art visuals to its soundtrack, nearly every aspect of the game appeals to my personal preferences and makes it an enjoyable experience to return to.

Passion Projects

Game Character Fanart

This illustration was part of my experimentation with different art styles. Since the character comes from a game and has a Chinese-inspired background and lore, I wanted to explore a traditional Chinese painting aesthetic for her artwork. She is known as the Phoenix Empress, which inspired me to incorporate elements that reflect the elegance and symbolism associated with her title. It was an opportunity for me to explore a different visual style while drawing inspiration from the character's identity and story.

Freelance Art Account

Around June 2026, I decided to start accepting commissions for digital illustrations, primarily character portraits. I created accounts across multiple platforms to share my artwork and reach potential clients. The image shown here is a screenshot of my TikTok account, which has become the platform where I receive most of my commission inquiries compared to my other accounts. Managing these accounts has also allowed me to share my work with a wider audience and gain experience as a freelance illustrator.

Halloween Fanart

This Halloween-themed illustration was posted as a speedpaint on my TikTok account on November 2, 2026. I had temporarily paused accepting commissions because of my busy school schedule, which gave me more time to create personal artwork without the pressure of client deadlines. The character is originally from a game, where she wields an umbrella as her weapon. For my Halloween redesign, I reimagined her weapon as a spiderweb and designed her outfit around the silhouette of a Victorian bustle dress, drawing inspiration from the appearance and characteristics of a black widow spider. This project allowed me to experiment with character redesign while creating something purely for my own enjoyment.

My passion projects may not be as extensive or elaborate as those of other artists, but they represent what I have been able to create with the limited time and resources available to me. Each project reflects something I've enjoyed, learned, or experimented with along the way. Regardless of their scale, I'm proud of what I've accomplished and hope to continue creating more whenever I have the opportunity.
